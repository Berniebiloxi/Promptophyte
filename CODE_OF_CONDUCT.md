# Code of Conduct

This project is built for people who are new to this. That only works if
everyone here treats the space as somewhere it's safe to not know things
yet. That's the whole rule. The rest of this document just spells it out.

## What we expect

- Assume the other person is asking in good faith and doing their best.
- Answer "obvious" questions without making anyone feel small for asking.
- Be patient with unclear reports — "I got lost here" is a valid start,
  and it's on us to ask follow-up questions kindly.
- Give feedback on someone's contribution, not on them.
- When you disagree, say why, and stay open to being wrong.

## What's not okay

- Personal attacks, insults, or demeaning comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private. This includes unwelcome
  attention, slurs, and deliberate intimidation.
- Discrimination or hostile comments based on who someone is — including
  their experience level.
- Sharing someone's private information without their clear permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behavior that breaks this, or that just makes the
space feel unsafe, contact the maintainer directly at
**berniebiloxi@gmail.com**. Reports are read privately. You don't need to
be the target to report something, and you won't be penalized for
reporting in good faith.

When a report comes in, the maintainer will look into it and decide what's
needed — that can range from a private word, to editing or removing
comments, to blocking someone from the project. The reporter's identity is
kept confidential.

## Where this applies

Everywhere connected to the project: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit comments, and any other project space. It also applies when someone
is representing the project in public.
